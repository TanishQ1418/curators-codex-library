PRIVACY POLICY & TERMS
Data We Store In Guest Mode, your codex is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. When signed in with Google, your library entries, social profile, friend relationships, and notifications are stored securely in Firebase Firestore under strict per-user access rules.
Public vs Private Data Your email address and friend invite code remain private to your account. New accounts default library visibility to Friends Only, which you can adjust anytime under Social Settings.
Your Control & Deletion You can export your complete library as JSON, switch visibility, or permanently erase your account and all cloud data from Social Settings → Delete My Account & Data.
